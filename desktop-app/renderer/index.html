2 · Auto-verify (optional)

Applies your buy box to each listing's agent remarks + condition + photo count: drops renovated / turnkey wording (Rule #0), multi-unit / second-unit, fire damage, and exterior-only (likely tenant); keeps genuine as-is / estate / fixer language. Judges from the listing's words, not the pixels — so a renovated home with plain remarks can slip through. For pixel-level judgement, add AI vision below.
Only applies without AI vision. The run never stops to ask you anything. The text rules read the agent remarks and never see a photo, so when the remarks say nothing about condition they cannot tell a renovated kitchen from an original one — your sheet reviewer is the backstop, and anything she rejects goes into the ledger so it never returns. Turn on AI vision below and this never comes up — it reads every photo and decides on its own.
Your key + photos go to Anthropic to apply the rules visually. claude-opus-5 is most accurate; claude-sonnet-5 / claude-haiku-4-5 cost less.

3 · Scan the buy box

Comps are off. The run stops at condition-qualified: scan → filter → photo review → sheet. Every listing that survives photo review goes to the sheet with its facts (price, $/sqft, sqft, beds, DOM) and no ARV or profit — comping means a second MLS search per listing and roughly triples the run. Tick the box when you want ARV, rehab, profit and the gate back.
One city at a time, in order. San Francisco is scanned, every new listing reviewed one by one, comped and pushed to the sheet — and only then does the next city start. Order: SF · whole Peninsula ($2M) · Sunnyvale · San Jose · Oakland · Berkeley · San Leandro · Hayward · Richmond. Active · SFR · any days on market.
