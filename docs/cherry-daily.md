# Cherry's Daily Page

Plain version. This is the whole job, in order.

## What your job is here

You make sure three things are true every day:

1. Every job has a photo.
2. Every dollar has an address.
3. Every hour has a photo behind it.

If those three are true, payroll is safe and Juan can make decisions. If one is
not true, you say so. You do not fix it yourself and you do not hide it.

## Your day

### Before 10:00 — check the photos

Look at every job that has a crew today. Each one needs an **arrival photo**:
front of the property, timestamp, and you can see who is there.

- Photo is there → good, move on.
- Photo is missing → send one message to the site lead: "No arrival photo at
  {address} yet. Please send one now." Then write the job down on your flag list.

You are not accusing anyone. You are asking for the photo.

### Around 11:00 — yesterday's hours

Open yesterday's timesheets. For each person, ask one question: **do the photos
cover the hours they claimed?**

- 8 hours claimed, photos show morning, midday and close → **PASS**.
- 8 hours claimed, only one photo at 9am → **HOLD**. Write the two numbers down:
  "clock 8.0, photos cover 3.0." Send it to Juan. Do not decide it yourself.
- No photos at all → **HOLD**, same thing.

A HOLD is not a punishment. It means one person needs to explain one day.

### Every day — the hour counts

Jairo and Alex (Alexander) are capped at **40 hours a week**.

Keep a running total for both. When either one hits **32 hours**, tell Juan and
the scheduler right away. Do not wait until 40 — by 40 it is too late to change
the schedule.

If one of them already worked the hours, **they still get paid.** That is the
law. You are stopping the schedule, not the paycheck. Tell Juan and let him
handle the conversation.

### Every day — receipts

Every receipt gets an address. Not "supplies." An address.

| What you need | Example |
|---|---|
| Property | 820 28th St |
| Date | 2026-09-16 |
| Vendor | Home Depot |
| Amount | $412.18 |
| Category | Lumber |
| Who bought it | Cesar |

If a receipt has no address and nobody can tell you which job it belongs to,
that is a **leak**. Write it on the leak list with the amount. Do not guess an
address. A guessed address is worse than a leak, because it hides the problem.

### End of day — the artifact

For each live property, run the daily prompt (`prompts/daily-artifact.md`) with
that day's photos, hours and receipts. Save what comes out.

Then send Juan **only the problems** — the holds, the mismatches, the leaks, and
anything about an agent. Not the full artifacts. He does not need to read six
pages. You keep the artifacts.

## If this happens, do this

| What happened | What you do |
|---|---|
| No arrival photo by 10:00 | Message the site lead. Flag the job. |
| Hours do not match photos | Write both numbers. Mark HOLD. Send to Juan. |
| Receipt with no address | Ask who bought it. If nobody knows → leak list. |
| Jairo or Alex hits 32 hours | Tell Juan and the scheduler today. |
| Jairo or Alex already went over 40 | They get paid. Tell Juan. Not a deduction. |
| An agent or inspector came to the job | Put the name on the Agent Radar. |
| Someone wants to buy something not on a job | Juan says yes first, in writing. |
| Same job misses photos three days running | That is a process problem. Tell Juan. |

## Friday

Run the weekly pulse (`prompts/weekly-pulse.md`). One page for Juan.

Three numbers lead it:

1. Cash out on jobs this week
2. Hours paid
3. Agents touched

Then: materials against budget by property, holds, leaks, and who on the agent
radar went quiet.

If you do not know a number, write **UNKNOWN**. Do not estimate. An estimate in
this page is worse than a blank, because Juan will treat it as real.

## Words we use

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Artifact** | The one-page daily pack for a property. Same shape every day. |
| **Hold** | Hours we are not running yet because something does not add up. |
| **Leak** | Money we spent that we cannot tie to a property. |
| **Photographer A / B** | The one person who takes job photos, plus one backup. |
| **Cap** | The 40-hour weekly limit on Jairo and Alex. |
| **Radar** | The list of San Francisco agents we are building a relationship with. |
| **Live job** | A property with a crew on it or money going out. |

## What you never have to do

- You never have to decide whether someone is lying. You write down what you see.
- You never have to guess an address. Unknown is an answer.
- You never have to chase a receipt more than once. Ask once, then flag it.
- You never have to send Juan everything. Exceptions only.
